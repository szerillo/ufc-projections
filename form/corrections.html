November 12, 2026 · Bilal Hasan's reach and his Form Line (UFC 334)

Bilal Hasan's reach was missing from our fighter data, so the Form model treated his reach as equal to Luis Gurule's. UFCStats and UFC.com both list it as 70 inches (Gurule: 64). With the reach included, Hasan's Form Line moves from 78.6% to 81.1%, public at 78.6% since November 10. The tale of the tape now shows his reach, and he now qualifies for the "Reach advantage 4"+" angle. His stance, and the reach of Bia Mesquita and Juan Diaz, stay "not listed": the two sources do not show matching values. No betting recommendation and no graded record changes.

November 12, 2026 · Form distance numbers for UFC 334

Two errors in the Form model's distance numbers, public since November 10:

Kayla Harrison vs Amanda Nunes is a five-round title fight, but its Form numbers were built as a three-round fight. "Goes the distance" was shown as 50.4%; on the five-round basis it is 43.0%.

Ciryl Gane vs Josh Hokit: interim heavyweight title fights were left out of the heavyweight history behind the division's decision rate. Counting them, "Goes the distance" moves from 28.1% to 27.5%.

Win percentages did not change. Across past cards, the same fix moves 16 fights' distance numbers by more than 1 point (8 by more than 3, all interim title fights); none of these feed a pick or a graded record.
