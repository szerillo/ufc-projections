What these numbers mean
Model
Our projection: the fair price and win chance for each side.
Listed
The price on our sheet when the projection was published.
Market
The current consensus price. The % is the straight conversion of that price, so it still includes the book's margin.
Edge
Model % minus the listed % (moneyline, goes the distance) or minus the market % (inside the distance, method). Positive means the model likes that side more than the price does.
Line move
How far the consensus win chance has moved since the line opened, with the opening price of the side it moved toward.
Striking angle
Shows when one fighter clears a striking gap we track: career strike differential 2+ per minute better, or round-3 differential about 3+ per minute better (both fighters with 4+ UFC fights). Tracked on paper, not a bet signal yet.
